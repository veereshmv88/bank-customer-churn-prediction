Methodology

Technical documentation of the machine learning pipeline, validation methodology, and responsible AI practices.

01 Project Objective

The objective is to accurately estimate individual customer churn probability (Exited = 1) and generate actionable operational risk scores (0–100) to support proactive retention workflows.

02 Dataset & Hygiene

Audited on 10,000 complete customer accounts (European_Bank.csv). Customer identifiers (CustomerId, Surname, Year) are strictly quarantined to eliminate target leakage.

03 Preprocessing & Feature Engineering

A Scikit-Learn ColumnTransformer is used for median imputation, standard scaling, and one-hot encoding. Five engineered features capture balance-salary concentration, relationship duration interaction, and product velocity.

04 Model Training & Evaluation

Five candidate models (Logistic Regression, Decision Tree, Random Forest, Gradient Boosting, XGBoost) were benchmarked via 5-Fold Stratified Cross-Validation on 8,000 training samples. The final tuned Gradient Boosting pipeline achieved 0.8690 ROC-AUC and 0.7184 PR-AUC on an untouched 2,000-sample test set.

05 Risk Scoring & Calibration

Linear transformation maps churn probability to a 100-point risk score: Risk Score = Churn Probability × 100. Provisional operational tiers: Low Risk (<25%), Medium Risk (25–49.9%), High Risk (≥50%).

06 Responsible Use & Governance

This system is an advisory decision-support tool. In accordance with banking regulations, it must never be used to enforce punitive terms, deny accounts, or restrict credit without human review.
